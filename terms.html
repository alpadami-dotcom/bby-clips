What this is

BBY Clips Uploader is a personal, non-commercial desktop tool run by a private individual. It uploads videos that the owner created to the owner's own TikTok account through the official TikTok API.

Who may use it

Only the owner. The tool is not offered, sold or distributed to anyone else, and no one else can sign in to it.

Content

Only content the owner made and has the right to post is uploaded. All posts must follow the TikTok Terms of Service and TikTok Community Guidelines. Each upload arrives as a draft; the owner reviews and publishes it in the TikTok app.

The tool is provided as is, without any warranty. It may stop working at any time, for example when the TikTok API changes.
