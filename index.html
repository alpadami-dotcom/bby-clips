BBY Clips Uploader

A personal desktop tool. It uploads gameplay clips that I recorded myself (EA SPORTS FC) from my own computer to my own TikTok account, as drafts in my TikTok inbox. I then finish and publish each post myself in the TikTok app.

It is not offered to anyone else and has no user accounts of its own.
